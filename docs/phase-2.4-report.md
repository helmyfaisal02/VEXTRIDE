# VEXTRIDE Phase 2.4 — City Environment Finalization

Implemented locally. Phases 1.1–2.3 were preserved. No vehicles or Phase 3 systems were added, and no staging, commit, push, deployment, authentication change, or blockchain operation was performed.

1. **Initial inspection**

   Started from a clean `main` tracking `origin/main`, at initial commit `49080f6`. The local Git identity matched the requested name and email. The existing city has a 340 × 340 m footprint, six districts, 40 roads, 80 right-hand lane edges, 20 buildings and 413 props. The initial lint, TypeScript and regression suite passed (554 PASS cases/groups).

   The city surface and road markings are two merged meshes. Raised sidewalk pieces were flat cuboids. Motor Row garage/showroom forecourts and parking pads were painted at road/lot level but interrupted by 0.13 m curbs. Environment physics already used one fixed body and 444 straightforward colliders; the city used another fixed body with 57 colliders. The old follow camera used a single ray, a forced 0.9 m minimum distance and damped inward correction that could temporarily leave the camera beyond the collision boundary.

3. **Driveway and curb-cut implementation**

   | Entrance | Sidewalk | Clear center / width | Destination |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Garage bay | B30-S | X = 102.2 m / 6 m | Existing right 5.2 m roll-up bay |
   | Showroom | B30-S | X = 130 m / 8 m | Existing showroom forecourt |
   | North parking | B30-N | X = 114 m / 6 m | Existing northern pad and central aisle |
   | South parking | B31-S | X = 124 m / 6 m | Existing southern pad and central aisle |

   Each center crossing is at road/lot height, 0.03 m, across the full 2.5 m sidewalk depth. This removes the curb barrier in both vehicle travel directions. Pedestrians retain a continuous sidewalk through each crossing: 1.5 m planar shoulders on either side reconnect it to the surrounding 0.16 m sidewalk. Their gradient is 0.13 / 1.5, approximately 8.7%, with no vertical step at either end. These are game traversal dimensions, not a claim of accessibility-code certification.

   The full-height collider is removed from each affected strip; the static triangle replacement is generated by the same functions used for visible geometry. Dense sampling found no holes or collision/mesh mismatches above 0.00002 m, with a measured maximum error of about 0.000000765 m. Surrounding sidewalk and the road lanes retain their original heights. Geometry is built at startup, never per frame.

   Parking paint now connects the existing pads to the lot-side ends of the curb cuts. Stall stripes are clipped out of each approach instead of advertising a parking space across the entrance. No road centerline, stop line, crosswalk or lane was changed. The garage remains closed and solid, as before; this phase prepares exterior access and does not add an interior or interactive door.

5. **Camera improvements**

   The follow camera now sweeps a reusable 0.24 m sphere with a 0.06 m skin. On a flat wall, the resulting approximately 0.30 m center clearance matches the previous ray margin, while grazing corners are detected reliably. Dynamic objects, kinematic objects and sensors remain excluded.

   The camera never eases beyond an obstruction. When the obstruction clears, it retains the existing exponential release rate of 5. Focus smoothing is constrained near corners, and the final rendered pose is checked after a creator/debug hand-over blend, preventing the blend itself from crossing a wall. Orbit parameters, drag/zoom input, the creator camera, C debug camera and transition timing were not redesigned.

   Tests include flat walls, grazing walls that a center ray misses, tight spaces below the old forced 0.9 m minimum, dynamic/sensor exclusion, unnecessary pull-in beside parallel walls, frame-rate-independent release, and 80 real building facade sweeps. All facade probes left the camera volume outside the colliders. Browser samples also found no camera-volume penetration while rotating beside the garage and an office.

   A genuinely obstructed camera angle can still require a close view of the character. Safety is prioritized over retaining a minimum distance that would put the camera inside a wall. Human acceptance of tight-wall framing and abrupt obstruction changes remains part of the checklist below.

8. **Performance considerations**

   City surface geometry increased from 658 to 898 triangles (+240). Road marking geometry remains 2,784 triangles. The city still uses two draw calls, and environment rendering retains six merged district chunks, instanced trees and its existing material/draw-call architecture. No geometry is rebuilt per frame and no new light is added.

   Camera queries reuse their sphere and vectors; at most three shape sweeps cover focus lag, desired placement and final blended placement. No additional physics bodies are created by the camera.

   The actual local browser used ANGLE/SwiftShader software rendering at 1280 × 800. One overlay sample measured **7 FPS, 147.7 ms, 74 calls, 31.9k triangles** at the tested view. Draw counts vary with the view and shadow passes. This is not a hardware benchmark or evidence that a mid-range desktop performance target has been achieved.
